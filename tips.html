Top 5 Cybersecurity Tips

1. Use Strong Passwords

Combine uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols to create complex passwords that are hard to guess.

2. Do Not Share Your Password

Keep your login credentials strictly confidential and never share them with anyone.

3. Do Not Click Suspicious Links

Avoid opening unfamiliar links or attachments sent via unexpected emails or messages to prevent phishing attacks.

4. Keep Your Software Updated

Regularly update your operating system and apps to patch security vulnerabilities and fix bugs.

5. Use Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Enable 2FA on your personal accounts to add an extra layer of security beyond just a password.
